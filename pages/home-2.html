OUR METHODOLOGY

The Fighter Protocol.

Four integrated phases designed to take lifters and beginners into complete physical conditioning.

01

Movement Screening

Comprehensive mobility, joint range, and kinetic power testing before any athlete hits heavy sparring or maximum loads.

02

Periodized S&C

Barbell mesocycles, sled sprints, and metabolic lactic conditioning to develop both explosive punching force and lung capacity.

03

Supervised Combat

Technical pad drills, cage wall tactics, and safety-first sparring clinics with certified referee coaching oversight.

04

Thermal Recovery

3°C cold plunge ice baths and dry cedar Finnish sauna protocols to suppress muscle soreness and accelerate recovery.

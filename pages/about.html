THE GENESIS · 2014

The antidote to commercial fitness culture.

Back in 2014, Bengaluru was flooded with franchise gyms with rows of treadmills, fragile plastic pin-select machines, and personal trainers pushing quick-fix detoxes. There was almost nowhere for serious lifters, martial artists, or athletes to test their limits under heavy barbells with chalk on their hands.

Mr Fit was founded on 100ft Road Indiranagar with a radical commitment: no shiny lobby gimmicks, no loud pop music playlists, and zero sales pressure. Just 4 Rogue power racks, 10 Texas power bars, calibrated steel plates, and passionate coaches who genuinely understood periodization and human biomechanics.

Zero Commercial Fluff Competition-grade barbells, calibrated iron & Olympic platforms
100% Coach-Led Standards Every session supervised by certified strength and combat practitioners
FLAGSHIP INDIRANAGAR The original barbell & combat proving ground since 2014.

OUR EVOLUTION

Twelve years of purposeful growth.

From an underground four-rack barbell rig to Bengaluru's most comprehensive athletic performance sanctuary.

2014

The Underground Rig

Started as a 2,500 sq.ft ground-floor warehouse in Indiranagar with 4 Rogue power racks, 10 Texas bars, and an initial cohort of 25 dedicated lifters.

2018

Combat Lab Integration

Installed our 16ft regulation octagon cage and 12 Fairtex leather heavy bags. Recruited national Muay Thai medalists to lead complete combat conditioning.

2021

Coaching Science Standard

Mandated CSCS (Strength & Conditioning) and ISSN (Sports Nutrition) credentials across all coaching staff, introducing velocity-based training protocols.

2023

Thermal Recovery Wing

Opened our contrast hydrotherapy wing featuring twin 3°C cold plunge ice baths and an 85°C Finnish cedar infrared sauna for central nervous system restoration.

2026 (Now)

Koramangala Mega-Facility

Launching our 10,000 sq.ft second branch on 80ft Road Koramangala with outdoor sprint turf, 8 Rogue rigs, laser speed gates, and open combat floor.
